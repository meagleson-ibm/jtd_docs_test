# Delete a Wafer Definition

Deleting a wafer definition allows a user to clear unused wafer definitions from the wafer management screen..

**Note:** Only users who are on teams assigned to the project that is referenced in a Wafer Definition can view, edit, delete, or download that Wafer Definition.

To navigate to the Wafer Management page:

-   From the Intelligent Design app, click the **Switcher** icon and select **Intelligent Test**. On the left side of the Intelligent Test page, click **Wafer Management**. The Wafer Management page opens.

1.  On the Wafer Management page, click **Hamburger Icon** for a given wafer definition and click **Delete** to open the Delete wafer definition dialogue. Multiple definitions can be deleted at once by checking the boxes by their name and clicking the **Delete** button that appears at the top of the Wafer definition list.

2.  On the delete dialogue, click **Delete**completely erase the selected wafer definitions and return to the Wafer Management page. Clicking **Cancel** navigates back to the Wafer Management page without erasing anything.
