# Deleting a Device

To Delete a Device, you must be on the Design View tab in a Project, and must be viewing the Device List.

There are two ways to Delete a Device. In the Device List and through the Edit Dialogue.

1.  On the **Design View** tabs, click the **Hamburger Icon** next to the device to be deleted and select Delete.

2.  Click **Delete** to delete the device

3.  To delete multiple Devices at once, click the **Check Box** to the left of one or more Devices' names.

4.  Click the **Delete** button that appears.

5.  Click **Delete** to delete the Device. Note that once deleted, a Device cannot be restored.
