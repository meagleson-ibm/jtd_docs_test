# Editing a Team

Editing a Team includes both changing the name and description of the Team, as well as adding and removing Collaborators..

**Note:** Only Project Administrators can create, edit, or delete Padsets

1.  Click your profile icon, then select **Administration configuration**.

2.  On the **Team Management** tab, click the **Hamburger Icon** and select **Edit**. to open the Edit team window

3.  On the **Attributes** tab, the Padset's Name and Description can be edited. Click **Save** to confirm any unsaved changes. This does not close the Edit Padset window.

4.  On the **Collaborators** tab, Click the **Add Collaborator** to open the Add Collaborator window.

5.  Click the **Check Box** next to the name of the Collaborators to add to the team, then click **Add** to add the Collaborators to the Team and return to the Collaborators tab.

6.  Certain Collaborators can have their role changed. To change a role, click the **Role** name and select a new one from the drop down list.

7.  To remove a Collaborator from the team, click the **Trash Can** icon by their name. To remove several Collaborators at once, use the **Check Boxes** by their names and click the **Trash Can** icon that appears at the top of the Collaborator list.

8.  Click **Save** to finish making changes to the team and close the Edit team window
