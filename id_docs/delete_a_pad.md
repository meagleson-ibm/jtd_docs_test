# Deleting a Pad

To Delete a Pad, you must be on the **Device Details Page**.

1.  Click the **Check Box** to the left of one or more Pads.

2.  Click the **Delete** button that appears.

3.  Click **Delete** to delete the Pad. Note that once deleted, a Pad cannot be restored.
