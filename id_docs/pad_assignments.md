# Pad Assignments

A Pad is a subsection of a Device, and is an even smaller area of the chip design.

Pad in Intelligent Design refers to a discrete area of metal with its own design, with the purpose of making contact with a probe during an electrical test. Pads are associated with both Devices and Padsets.

When viewing a Project, click on a **Device** tab to see what Pads, if any, are part of that Device.
