# Deleting a Team

**Note:** Only Project Administrators can create, edit, or delete a team.

1.  Click your profile icon, then select **Administration configuration**.

2.  On the **Team Management** tab, click the **Hamburger Icon** and select **Delete**

3.  On the Delete team dialogue, click **Delete** to confirm and delete the team. Deleting a team will also unlink it's Collaborators from the projects assigned to it. A team cannot be restored once deleted.
