# Team Management

Team Management in Intelligent Design involves assigning employees to teams for assignment to projects.

A Team is a collection of Collaborators or employees in Intelligent Design, all of whom must have a valid login. Each Collaborator can be assigned to multiple teams at once.

**Note:** Only Project Administrators can create, edit, or delete Teams.

-   **[Assigning a Team to a Project](../id_docs/add_team_to_project.md)**
