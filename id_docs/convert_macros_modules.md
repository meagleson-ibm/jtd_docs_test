# Coverting Macros into Modules

Project Administrators, Macro owners and Designers can convert macros that they created to modules.

Project Administrators, Macro owners and Designers can convert macros that they created in a project to modules that are equal or smaller.

1.  Select the **Project** tab where you created your macros.

    The **Project** page opens and displays two tabs, the **Design View** and **Macro View**.

2.  Click **Macro** View and scroll to the Macro that you created.

3.  Click the check box next to the Macro name.

4.  Click **More** and then click **Convert into modules** in the menu bar.

5.  On the Convert macro into modules window, select the layout that you want to convert.

6.  Click **Create Modules**.

    A system notification confirms that your Macros were converted into modules.
