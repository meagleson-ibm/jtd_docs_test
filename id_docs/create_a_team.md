# Creating a Team

**Note:** Only Project Administrators can create, edit, or delete Teams

1.  Click your profile icon, then select **Administration configuration**.

2.  On the **Team Management** tab, click **Create team**.

3.  Type a unique **Team name**. This must be unique across the entire Intelligent Design application, but may be up to 500 characters long.

4.  Enter a **Description** for the Team. This may be up to 1000 characters.

5.  Click **Next** to proceed to the **Collaborators** tab.

6.  Click the **Add Collaborator** button to open the Add Collaborator window.

7.  Click the **Check Box** next to the name of the Collaborators to add to the team, then click **Add** to add the Collaborators to the Team and return to the Collaborators tab.

8.  Certain Collaborators can have their role changed. To change a role, click the **Role** name and select a new one from the drop down list.

9.  Click **Next** to proceed to the Review and Save tab.

10. Review the Team Name, Description and Collaborators for accuracy, then click **Create**.
