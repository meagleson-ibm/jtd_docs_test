# Transient objects \(Draft reports\)

When you create reports in ABI, they are temporarily saved in DRAFT status, and can be found in the **Drafts** folder on the **Reports & Portfolios** tab.

Draft reports and other transient objects will be deleted after a specified number of days. To permanently save a report, you must click **Save** on the report details page.

There are two type of transient objects:

1.  Draft reports.
2.  Other temporary objects created, such as drill down BOX reports from ITL reports.

By Default, draft reports are deleted after 7 days. Other temporary objects are deleted after 2 days.
